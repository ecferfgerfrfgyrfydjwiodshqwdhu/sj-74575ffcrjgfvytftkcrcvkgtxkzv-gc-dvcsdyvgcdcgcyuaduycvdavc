About Us

About Startup Jaipur

A comprehensive incubation program designed to transform the startup dreams of youth into solid reality.

StartupJaipur

Startup Jaipur is a comprehensive startup program and incubator built for anyone with the ambition to build their own startup. Our main goal is to create a complete ecosystem where a small idea can be nurtured into a highly successful startup.

Building a startup today isn't easy. A new founder faces challenges with legal paperwork, company registration, tech development, and most importantly—Funding. We are here to solve exactly these problems.

We provide 360-Degree End-to-End Support. If you only have an idea, our team will guide you on how to execute it. If your product is ready, we will fully assist you in raising funds from Government Grants (like iStart Rajasthan) and private investors.

The Core Mission: Humara lakshya Jaipur ko agla bada Tech aur Innovation Hub banana hai. Aksar founders ko lagta hai ki bada startup banane ke liye Bangalore ya Gurgaon jana zaroori hai. Startup Jaipur is soch ko badal raha hai. Hum wo saari world-class facilities, mentorship, aur funding opportunities yahin Pink City mein laa rahe hain.

What Makes Us Different? Zyada tar programs sirf basic advice dete hain. Lekin Startup Jaipur ek 'Hands-On' accelerator hai. Iska matlab hai ki hum aapke startup mein aapke partner ki tarah kaam karte hain. Agar aapko website ya app banwani hai, hamari in-house tech team banayegi. Agar company register (Pvt Ltd) karni hai, hamare CAs karenge. Aapka poora focus sirf apne product aur growth par hona chahiye, baaki sab hum sambhalenge.

Who is this program for?
🎓 Students & Grads: Who have innovative tech ideas and want to build the future. 👩‍💼 Women Entrepreneurs: Looking to launch their own D2C brands, boutiques, or tech startups. 🏪 Traditional Entrepreneurs: Who want to scale their local/family setups into digital tech startups using E-commerce and ONDC. 💡 Early-stage Innovators: Jinke paas abhi sirf ek rough idea hai aur unhe market validation ki zaroorat hai.

The Ecosystem Power: Jab aap Startup Jaipur join karte hain, toh aapko access milta hai hamare exclusive network ka. Regular networking events, mock investor pitch sessions, aur 'Demo Days' ke through aapko seedha Angel Investors aur Venture Capitalists (VCs) ke samne apna idea rakhne ka mauka milta hai.

By combining the traditional Marwari startup mindset of Rajasthan with today's modern technology, we are building Jaipur into the next major Tech Hub. We refuse to let any great idea die simply due to a lack of capital or proper guidance.

"Our dream is to ensure that a normal student or entrepreneur from Jaipur gets the exact same resources, global network, and capital access as someone from a top metro city or elite institute. You bring the passion; we will provide the platform."

The Complete Ecosystem to Build Your Startup

Aapko kahin aur jaane ki jarurat nahi hai. Startup Jaipur ki team aapko zero se lekar success tak guide karegi. Pura process hum sambhalenge!

Step 01

Business Idea & Name

Right business idea choose karna aur uska perfect name analysis karna humari zimmedari.

Ek successful startup ki shuruaat ek solid idea aur catchy naam se hoti hai. Startup Jaipur ki team aapke interest aur market demand ke hisaab se profitable business ideas filter karke degi. Hum proper market research aur competitor analysis karenge. Uske baad, ek unique, yaad rakhne layak brand name suggest karenge jiska .com domain aur social media handles easily mil jayein. Hum trademark availability bhi check karenge taaki future mein koi legal issue na aaye.

Step 02

Terms & Conditions

1. Program Eligibility:

To apply for the Startup Jaipur incubation program, the founder must be at least 14 years of age, and the startup idea must be legally valid and compliant with Indian laws.

2. Code of Conduct:

Maintaining discipline and professional behavior during all online/offline mentorship sessions and networking events is mandatory. Any form of misbehavior or harassment will not be tolerated.

3. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA):

We keep your startup ideas strictly confidential. However, any information shared by you during public pitch days or networking events will be considered open to the public.

4. Selection & Rejection:

The Startup Jaipur management team reserves the absolute right to accept or reject any startup application based on our internal evaluation criteria.

5. Termination:

If any startup is found engaging in fraudulent activities, illegal practices, or violating program rules, their incubation and all associated benefits will be terminated immediately.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our utmost priority. Startup Jaipur is fully committed to keeping your personal data and startup details secure.

Data Collection:

When you register, we collect essential information such as your name, email address, mobile number, and pitch deck to properly evaluate your startup idea.

Data Usage:

Your data is solely used to connect you with industry mentors, assist you in applying for Government grants (like iStart Rajasthan), and provide personalized incubation services.

Data Sharing:

We do not sell or rent your data to any third-party marketing agencies. Data is only shared with trusted investors, mentors, or Government officials directly involved in your funding and growth process.

Security:

All your sensitive documents and confidential startup details are stored in an encrypted format on our secure cloud servers.

Refund Policy

1. Free Incubation Services:

Startup Jaipur's core incubation program, expert mentorship, and Government grant assistance are completely FREE and operate on a Zero-Equity model. Therefore, no payments or refunds are applicable for these core services.

2. Premium/Paid IT Services:

If you voluntarily opt for any paid premium services from the Startup Jaipur IT panel, such as Website Development, App Development, or customized CA Services, the upfront payment is strictly non-refundable as technical resources and manpower are allocated immediately upon confirmation.

3. Payment Failures:

In the rare event that a double payment is deducted from your account due to a technical glitch or server timeout, the excess amount will be automatically refunded to your original source account within 5-7 working days.

4. Dispute:

For any serious disputes regarding premium service deliverables, founders are encouraged to reach out to our official grievance redressal team for an amicable resolution.

Grievance Redressal

If you have any complaints or concerns regarding any of Startup Jaipur's programs, mentors, or associated premium services, we are fully committed to addressing and resolving them promptly.
